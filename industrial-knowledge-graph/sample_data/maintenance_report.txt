1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
This maintenance report documents the inspection, fault identification, and 
corrective maintenance activities performed on critical equipment at the North 
Processing Facility during the scheduled quarterly maintenance shutdown.

2. EQUIPMENT UNDER INSPECTION
------------------------------

2.1 Pump P-101 - Centrifugal Process Pump
Equipment ID: P-101
Location: Pump Station A, North Plant
Type: Centrifugal Pump
Serial Number: CP-2019-4521
Last Maintenance: October 10, 2023

FINDINGS:
Pump P-101 developed excessive vibration during normal operation. The vibration 
levels measured 12.5 mm/s RMS, exceeding the alarm threshold of 7.1 mm/s.
Inspection revealed that the bearing failure was the root cause of the excessive 
vibration. The radial bearing B-401 showed significant wear and had developed 
cracks due to lack of lubrication.

The pump P-101 is driven by Motor M-201 through a flexible coupling. The 
coupling alignment was found to be misaligned by 0.3 mm, which also contributed 
to the bearing failure.

Pump P-101 has a Temperature Sensor T-501 installed on the bearing housing. 
The temperature sensor T-501 recorded temperatures of 95 degrees C, well above 
the normal operating range of 60 to 70 degrees C.

MAINTENANCE ACTIONS PERFORMED:
- Bearing B-401 replacement with new SKF 6205 bearing
- Coupling realignment to within 0.05 mm tolerance
- Lubrication of all bearing housings with Mobil SHC 630 grease
- Mechanical seal replacement on Pump P-101
- Impeller inspection - minor erosion noted, within acceptable limits

SPARE PARTS USED:
- SKF 6205 radial bearing (2 units)
- Mechanical seal kit (1 set)
- Coupling insert (1 unit)

STATUS: Pump P-101 returned to service after maintenance. Vibration levels 
reduced to 2.3 mm/s RMS. Temperature readings normalized to 62 degrees C.

================================================================================

2.2 Motor M-201 - Electric Drive Motor
Equipment ID: M-201
Location: Pump Station A, North Plant
Type: Induction Motor, 45 kW, 1450 RPM, 415V
Serial Number: EM-2018-7823

FINDINGS:
Motor M-201 drives Pump P-101 and was inspected as part of the pump maintenance 
activity. The motor winding insulation resistance tested at 85 MOhm, within 
acceptable limits. The motor bearings were lubricated and checked for excessive 
wear.

Motor M-201 rotor showed no signs of damage. The stator winding temperature was 
recorded at 75 degrees C during normal operation, within design limits.

The motor cooling fan was cleaned of dust accumulation which had been causing 
overheating risk.

MAINTENANCE ACTIONS PERFORMED:
- Motor bearing lubrication
- Cooling fan cleaning
- Terminal connection tightening
- Insulation resistance testing

STATUS: Motor M-201 is in good condition and returned to normal operation.

================================================================================

2.3 Valve V-301 - Control Valve
Equipment ID: V-301
Location: Discharge Line, Pump Station A
Type: Globe Control Valve, DN150, Class 300
Actuator: Pneumatic Actuator

FINDINGS:
Valve V-301 showed internal leakage due to seat erosion. The valve was 
disassembled for inspection. The valve seat showed erosion damage caused by 
cavitation in the process line. The valve plug also showed signs of erosion.

The actuator diaphragm of Valve V-301 was found to be damaged, causing 
inconsistent valve positioning.

MAINTENANCE ACTIONS PERFORMED:
- Valve seat lapping and reconditioning
- Valve plug replacement
- Actuator diaphragm replacement
- Valve packing replacement
- Calibration and positioner adjustment

SPARE PARTS USED:
- Valve plug assembly (1 unit)
- Actuator diaphragm kit (1 set)
- Packing set (1 set)

STATUS: Valve V-301 returned to service with improved performance. Internal 
leakage reduced to within acceptable limits.

3. SAFETY PROCEDURES FOLLOWED
-------------------------------
All maintenance activities were performed following the plant Safety Operating 
Procedure SOP-MAINT-001. 

Before any maintenance work:

- Safety permits were obtained from the safety department
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) including safety goggles, gloves, and

The maintenance team followed the standard safety procedure for confined space

4. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------------------------
Based on this inspection, the following preventive maintenance activities are 
recommended:

1. Pump P-101: Increase lubrication frequency from quarterly to monthly for 
   bearing B-401. Install vibration monitoring system for continuous monitoring.

2. Motor M-201: Schedule annual insulation resistance testing. Clean cooling 
   fan every 6 months to prevent overheating.

3. Valve V-301: Investigate cavitation in the process line upstream of the 
   valve. Consider installing a cavitation-resistant trim.

4. Install online vibration sensor on Pump P-101 bearing housing for 
   predictive maintenance.
