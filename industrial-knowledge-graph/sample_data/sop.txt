SOP: PUMP P-101 STARTUP, OPERATION AND SHUTDOWN
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
---------------------
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) provides instructions for the safe 
startup, normal operation, and shutdown of Pump P-101 and associated equipment 
including Motor M-201, Valve V-301, and monitoring instruments.

This SOP applies to all operators and technicians responsible for operating 
Pump P-101 at the North Processing Facility.

2. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
HAZARD IDENTIFICATION:
- Rotating machinery: Pump P-101 and Motor M-201 have rotating parts
- High temperature fluid: Process fluid temperature up to 85 degrees C
- High pressure: Operating pressure up to 6.5 bar
- Electrical hazard: Motor M-201 operates at 415V, 3-phase

PPE REQUIREMENTS:
Before approaching Pump P-101 or Motor M-201:
- Wear safety helmet
- Wear safety goggles
- Wear heat-resistant gloves when handling hot surfaces
- Wear safety boots
- Wear hearing protection (area noise > 85 dB)

LOCKOUT/TAGOUT:
The Lockout Tagout (LOTO) procedure SOP-LOTO-001 must be followed before any 
maintenance work on Pump P-101, Motor M-201, or Valve V-301.

3. PRE-STARTUP CHECKS
-----------------------
Before starting Pump P-101, the operator must complete the following checks:

3.1 Visual Inspection of Pump P-101:
  a) Check that all access panels and guards are in place
  b) Check for any signs of fluid leakage around the mechanical seal
  c) Check that coupling guard between Motor M-201 and Pump P-101 is installed
  d) Check pump P-101 foundation bolts are tight
  e) Check bearing housing oil/grease level is adequate
  f) Check bearing B-401 condition (no unusual noise)

3.2 Instrument Checks:
  a) Verify Temperature Sensor T-501 is reading ambient temperature (no fault)
  b) Verify Vibration Sensor VS-701 shows zero reading (pump stopped)
  c) Check suction and discharge pressure gauges PG-601 are functioning
  d) Verify all alarm setpoints in DCS are set correctly:
     - Temperature High Alarm (T-501): 80 degrees C
     - Vibration High Alarm (VS-701): 7.1 mm/s
  
3.3 Electrical Checks for Motor M-201:
  a) Verify Motor M-201 power supply is available
  b) Check motor overload protection is set to 85 A
  c) Verify motor thermal protection is enabled
  d) Check motor is not in lockout tagout

3.4 Valve Position Verification:
  a) Ensure Valve V-301 (discharge control valve) is CLOSED
  b) Ensure suction isolation valve is OPEN
  c) Ensure all drain valves are CLOSED
  d) Ensure vent valves are CLOSED after priming

3.5 Priming Pump P-101:
  a) Open suction isolation valve fully
  b) Slowly open vent valve on pump casing
  c) Wait until fluid flows from vent (no air present)
  d) Close vent valve

4. STARTUP PROCEDURE FOR PUMP P-101
--------------------------------------
STEP 1: Confirm all pre-startup checks are complete.

STEP 2: Notify the Control Room that Pump P-101 startup is in progress.

STEP 3: Start Motor M-201:
  a) Ensure Valve V-301 is in closed position (0%)
  b) Start Motor M-201 from local control panel or DCS
  c) Motor M-201 starting current is approximately 500 A (6 times FLC)
  d) Starting current should reduce to normal within 5 seconds
  e) Verify Motor M-201 speed reaches 1450 RPM

STEP 4: Monitor Pump P-101 during startup:
  a) Listen for unusual noises from pump bearings
  b) Check vibration sensor VS-701 reading is below 4.5 mm/s
  c) Check temperature sensor T-501 reading - should be near ambient initially

STEP 5: Slowly open Valve V-301 to establish flow:
  a) Gradually open V-301 from 0% to 25% over 30 seconds
  b) Check discharge pressure should rise to 5.0 to 6.5 bar
  c) Continue opening V-301 to required flow rate
  d) Target flow rate: 100 to 130 m3/h
  e) Monitor suction pressure - should remain above 0.5 bar to prevent cavitation

STEP 6: Verify normal operating conditions:
  a) Discharge Pressure: 5.0 to 6.5 bar ✓
  b) Flow Rate: 100 to 130 m3/h ✓
  c) Bearing Temperature (T-501): Below 70 degrees C ✓
  d) Vibration Level (VS-701): Below 4.5 mm/s ✓
  e) Motor Current (M-201): 75 to 85 A ✓

STEP 7: Notify Control Room that Pump P-101 is in normal operation.

5. NORMAL OPERATION MONITORING
---------------------------------
During normal operation, operators must check Pump P-101 system every 2 hours:

5.1 Parameters to Monitor:
  - Temperature sensor T-501 reading (Normal: 50-70 degrees C)
  - Vibration sensor VS-701 reading (Normal: < 4.5 mm/s)
  - Pump P-101 flow rate (Normal: 100-130 m3/h)
  - Discharge pressure (Normal: 5.0-6.5 bar)
  - Motor M-201 current (Normal: 75-85 A)
  - Motor M-201 temperature (Normal: < 85 degrees C)
  - Mechanical seal condition - no visible leakage

5.2 Alarm Response:
  HIGH TEMPERATURE ALARM (T-501 > 80 degrees C):
  - Check bearing lubrication
  - Increase monitoring frequency
  - Notify Maintenance Department
  - If temperature continues to rise, prepare for shutdown

  HIGH VIBRATION ALARM (VS-701 > 7.1 mm/s):
  - Check for unusual noises
  - Check coupling condition
  - Notify Maintenance Department immediately
  - Prepare for emergency shutdown if vibration increases

  LOW FLOW ALARM:
  - Check Valve V-301 position
  - Check suction strainer for blockage
  - Check suction pressure

6. NORMAL SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
-------------------------------
STEP 1: Notify Control Room that Pump P-101 shutdown is in progress.

STEP 2: Slowly close Valve V-301 to reduce flow:
  a) Gradually close V-301 from operating position to 0% over 60 seconds
  b) This prevents water hammer in the process line

STEP 3: Stop Motor M-201:
  a) Stop Motor M-201 from local panel or DCS
  b) Pump P-101 should coast to stop within 30 seconds
  c) Verify vibration sensor VS-701 drops to zero

STEP 4: Post-shutdown checks:
  a) Check that Pump P-101 has stopped completely
  b) Verify no fluid leakage from mechanical seal
  c) Record shutdown time and equipment readings in log book

STEP 5: Notify Control Room that shutdown is complete.

7. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
----------------------------------
In case of emergency (major leak, fire, high temperature trip, high vibration trip):

STEP 1: Press the EMERGENCY STOP button on Motor M-201 local panel.

STEP 2: Immediately close Valve V-301 manually (close isolation valve if necessary).

STEP 3: Evacuate the area if there is a major fluid release or fire.

STEP 4: Activate plant emergency alarm system.

STEP 5: Contact Control Room and Safety Department immediately.

STEP 6: Do NOT restart Pump P-101 until the cause of the emergency is identified 
        and corrective action has been taken by the Maintenance Team.

8. POST-MAINTENANCE STARTUP
-----------------------------
After any maintenance work on Pump P-101 or Motor M-201:

1. Verify LOTO has been properly removed following SOP-LOTO-001
2. Verify all maintenance work is complete and documented
3. Check that all tools and materials have been removed from the area
4. Ensure Bearing B-401 has been properly lubricated
5. Verify coupling alignment has been checked after reinstallation
6. Complete all pre-startup checks listed in Section 3
7. Perform startup as per Section 4

9. RECORD KEEPING
------------------
The following records must be maintained for Pump P-101:
- Equipment log book: Record all readings every 2 hours during operation
- Maintenance records: Document all maintenance activities
- Alarm history: Record all alarms and responses
- Operating hours: Track total operating hours for maintenance planning

Records must be retained for minimum 3 years.
