================================================================================

SECTION 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW
----------------------------
The Pump P-101 is a horizontal centrifugal pump designed for continuous process 
fluid transfer in the North Processing Facility. The pump is driven by Motor M-201, 
a 45 kW induction motor, through a flexible coupling.

System Components:
- Pump P-101: Horizontal centrifugal pump
- Motor M-201: 45 kW electric induction motor
- Bearing B-401: Radial rolling element bearing (SKF 6205 series)
- Mechanical Seal: Single mechanical seal (John Crane Type 1)
- Coupling: Flexible jaw coupling (Lovejoy L-100)
- Valve V-301: Discharge control valve
- Temperature Sensor T-501: PT100 RTD on bearing housing
- Pressure Gauge PG-601: Suction and discharge pressure gauges
- Vibration Sensor VS-701: Accelerometer on bearing housing

SECTION 2: TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------
Pump P-101 Technical Data:
  Type: Horizontal centrifugal pump

SECTION 3: COMPONENT DESCRIPTIONS
------------------------------------

3.1 Bearing B-401
The radial bearing B-401 supports the pump shaft and must withstand both radial 
and axial loads during operation. The bearing B-401 is a deep groove ball bearing 
(SKF 6205) with the following specifications:
  Bore Diameter: 25 mm
  Outer Diameter: 52 mm
  Width: 15 mm
  Dynamic Load Rating: 14.0 kN
  Static Load Rating: 7.8 kN
  Maximum Speed: 13000 RPM

The bearing B-401 requires grease lubrication with Mobil SHC 630 or equivalent. 
Lubrication interval: every 2000 operating hours or 3 months, whichever comes first.

Bearing B-401 is part of Pump P-101 and is located in the bearing housing on 
the drive end of the pump shaft.

3.2 Mechanical Seal
The mechanical seal prevents process fluid leakage along the pump shaft. The seal 
is a John Crane Type 1 single mechanical seal. The seal is used in Pump P-101 to 
seal the pump casing.

Seal face material: Carbon vs Silicon Carbide
O-ring material: EPDM (for water service) or Viton (for chemical service)

3.3 Temperature Sensor T-501
The temperature sensor T-501 is a PT100 RTD (Resistance Temperature Detector) 
installed on the bearing housing of Pump P-101. The sensor T-501 measures the 
bearing temperature to detect overheating conditions.

The temperature sensor T-501 is connected to the plant DCS (Distributed Control 
System) and generates:
  - High Temperature Alarm: 80 degrees C
  - High High Temperature Trip: 95 degrees C

The sensor T-501 measures bearing temperature during continuous operation.

3.4 Vibration Sensor VS-701
The vibration sensor VS-701 is an accelerometer installed on the bearing housing 
of Pump P-101. The sensor VS-701 measures vibration levels to detect mechanical 
problems such as bearing failure, misalignment, or imbalance.

Vibration Alarm Level: 7.1 mm/s RMS
Vibration Trip Level: 11.2 mm/s RMS

SECTION 4: INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------------
Pump P-101 must be installed on a concrete foundation with adequate mass to 
minimize vibration. The foundation must be level within 0.1 mm per meter.

Motor M-201 and Pump P-101 must be aligned within the following tolerances:
  Angular misalignment: Maximum 0.05 degrees
  Parallel offset: Maximum 0.05 mm

The coupling between Motor M-201 and Pump P-101 must be properly aligned to 
prevent excessive vibration and bearing wear.

SECTION 5: NORMAL OPERATING PARAMETERS
-----------------------------------------
Pump P-101 Normal Operating Conditions:
  Suction Pressure: 0.5 to 2.0 bar
  Discharge Pressure: 5.0 to 6.5 bar
  Flow Rate: 100 to 130 m3/h
  Bearing Temperature (T-501): 50 to 70 degrees C
  Vibration (VS-701): Less than 4.5 mm/s RMS
  Motor Current (M-201): 75 to 85 A

SECTION 6: FAULT CONDITIONS AND TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------

6.1 Excessive Vibration
FAULT: Pump P-101 excessive vibration (VS-701 reading > 7.1 mm/s)
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
  - Bearing failure (bearing B-401 worn or damaged)
  - Coupling misalignment between Motor M-201 and Pump P-101
  - Impeller damage or imbalance
  - Cavitation in the pump
  - Foundation looseness

CORRECTIVE ACTION:
  1. Check vibration spectrum to identify dominant frequency
  2. Check bearing condition - replace bearing B-401 if worn
  3. Check and correct coupling alignment
  4. Check impeller for damage or erosion
  5. Check suction conditions to prevent cavitation

6.2 High Bearing Temperature
FAULT: Temperature sensor T-501 reading > 80 degrees C
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
  - Insufficient lubrication
  - Bearing B-401 failure
  - Excessive load
  - Cooling air restriction on Motor M-201

CORRECTIVE ACTION:
  1. Check lubrication level and quality
  2. Inspect bearing B-401 for damage
  3. Check motor cooling fan is clear

6.3 Reduced Flow Rate
FAULT: Flow rate below design value (< 100 m3/h)
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
  - Worn or damaged impeller
  - Partially closed Valve V-301
  - Pump P-101 suction line blocked
  - Air in suction line

CORRECTIVE ACTION:
  1. Check Valve V-301 position
  2. Check suction strainer for blockage
  3. Inspect impeller for wear or damage

SECTION 7: MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
----------------------------------
Daily Checks:
  - Check vibration sensor VS-701 reading
  - Check temperature sensor T-501 reading
  - Check pump P-101 for unusual noises
  - Check motor M-201 temperature
  - Check for fluid leaks around mechanical seal

Monthly Maintenance:
  - Lubricate bearing B-401 with Mobil SHC 630 grease
  - Check coupling condition
  - Check mechanical seal for leakage

Quarterly Maintenance:
  - Complete inspection of Pump P-101 and Motor M-201
  - Alignment check between motor and pump
  - Bearing B-401 inspection - replace if necessary
  - Impeller inspection
  - Valve V-301 inspection and calibration

Annual Maintenance:
  - Complete overhaul of Pump P-101
  - Motor M-201 insulation resistance testing
  - Replacement of mechanical seal as preventive measure
  - Bearing B-401 replacement as preventive measure

SECTION 9: SAFETY INFORMATION
--------------------------------
WARNING: Before performing any maintenance on Pump P-101 or Motor M-201, 
implement Lockout Tagout (LOTO) procedure as per SOP-LOTO-001.

CAUTION: Pump P-101 may contain hot and pressurized process fluid. Always 
depressurize and cool the system before opening.

PPE Requirements:
  - Safety helmet
  - Safety goggles
  - Chemical resistant gloves
  - Safety boots with steel toe cap
  - Hearing protection (noise level > 85 dB in pump area)

Emergency Procedure:
  In case of major seal failure or catastrophic pump failure:
  1. Immediately close Valve V-301 (discharge isolation valve)
  2. Stop Motor M-201 using emergency stop button
  3. Evacuate the area
  4. Contact Plant Control Room and Safety Department
  5. Follow emergency response procedure ERP-PUMP-001
